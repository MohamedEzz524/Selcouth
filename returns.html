Last updated · 29 July 2026

Time limits

Exchange within 3 days of the delivery date. Return within 1 day of the delivery date.

Fragrance is a consumable product, so an item cannot be returned or exchanged once it has been used — even a single time — or once its original wrapping has been opened.

Shipping on returns

Where you wish to exchange or return an item after delivery, the shipping cost for both the returned order and the replacement order is payable by you. That amount goes directly to the courier.

If your order has already left with the courier, or a waybill has been issued, and you then ask to cancel, the value of the product is refunded less the delivery charge — this applies even where the order was placed with free shipping.

Damaged items

If an item arrives damaged, it is replaced with the same product. Please attach photographs of the damaged item. Compensation is made within 15 days of the delivery date.

Orders we may not accept

The store may accept, decline or cancel an order in certain cases — for example where the product is unavailable, where it was priced incorrectly, or where the order is found to be fraudulent. Anything paid for an order that is not accepted, or that is cancelled, is refunded.
