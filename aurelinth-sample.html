Fragrance family: Citrus · Floral · Musky · Woody

A floral oriental in the gourmand family with a boozy touch, built around natural yellow flowers — honeyed linden blossom at its heart and smoky broom. It moves from a bright, spicy freshness into a rum-soaked white floral bouquet, then settles into a sweet, woody warmth. Complex and very wearable, in every season.

Naturals 28% · Concentration 25% · 3 ML

Top / Linden blossom, Bergamot, Ginger, Boozy notes

Heart / Linden blossom, Ylang ylang, Heliotrope, Osmanthus, Tuberose, Broom, Frangipani, Rum

Base / Virginia cedar, Musk, Burnt match, Sandalwood, Moss, Honey, Haitian vetiver

UNISEX · EXTRAIT · SMALL BATCH

Key raw materials: Linden blossom extract (France), Broom extract (France), Ginger oil (Madagascar), Sicilian bergamot, Rum musk (India), Ylang ylang (Madagascar), Pink lotus (China), Frangipani (India), Virginia cedarwood, Australian sandalwood, Haitian vetiver.
